# Lab 1 - Phishing Attacks

## What is a Phishing Attack?

A phishing attack is a type of social **engineering attack** where an attacker pretends to be a trustworthy person or organization to trick someone into:

- Revealing sensitive information
- Clicking a malicious link
- Opening a malicious attachment
- Performing other actions which benefit the attacker

Think of phishing as **deception rather than a technical attack**. This is because it doesn't involve breaking into a system, but rather **manipulating a victim into helping the attacker**.

## What are the different types of Phishing Attacks?

Common phishing methods include:

| Method | Description |
|--------|---------|
|Email phishing|Fraudulent emails pretending to be legitimate organizations.|
|Spear phishing|Targeted phishing aimed at a specific person or organization. For example, an attacker researches an employee and then sends a fake email requesting the employee to reset their password.|
|Smishing|Phishing through SMS/text messages. |
| Vishing | Phishing through voice calls. For example scam calls, trying to verify banking details. |
| Malicious attachments | Files designed to deliver malware when opened.|

## Recent Attacks

There have been several notable phishing campaigns in 2026 alone.

### 1. AI-themed ChatGPT phishing - May 2026

Microsoft detected a campaign pretending to be **ChatGPT**. The email claimed the recipient needed to update their ChatGPT Plus payment method or their account would be downgraded.

The phishing page collected **credit-card and personal information**. Microsoft observed one campaign sending 4,500 emails to targets in South Africa, while related activity reached as many as 100,000 emails in a single day across Switzerland, Austria and South Africa.

### 2. Executive impersonation and fake invoices - August 2026

Between 3-5 August 2026, Microsoft detected more than one million emails targeting enterprise users.

The attackers in question, impersonated senior executives such as CEOs, CFOs and presidents. They also used fake vendor branding and fraudulent invoices to persuade employees to make ACH payments of around $50,000 to an attacker-controlled account.

### 3. HRMC Tax-Refund Phishing - currently ongoing in the UK

HMRC continues to deal with large volumes of phishing and other scams impersonating the department.

In the 2025-2026 **financial year**, HMRC responded to 134,309 reports of suspicious contact from the public, including 25,398 bogus tax-rebate messages.

## Phishing Tools

Phishing tools are software tools that can help attackers create, send, manage and/or improve phishing attacks. These same tools can also be used by cybersecurity professionals to simulate phishing attacks and train employees.

| Type of tool | Purpose | Example |
|---------|-------|----------|
|Phishing campaign platforms|Creates and sends simulated phishing emails and tracks who accesses them.|Gophish|
|Phishing page generators|This tool creates fake login page/s that imitate legitimate websites.|Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET)|
|Credential/session phishing frameworks|Attempts to intercept authentication information or sessions.|Evilgnix|
| Email spoofing | Helps make emails appear to come from another sender.| Various email infrastructure/tools. |
| Phishing kits | Pre-built packages containing fake websites, emails and other components.| Criminally distributed phishing kits. |
